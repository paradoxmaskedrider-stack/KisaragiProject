TacticalBackpack commit40
=========================

ForgeGradle 7 / Gradle 9.8.0 AE2 development-runtime fix.

Changes:
- AE2 and GuideME are now declared with renamer.dependency(...), not plain implementation.
- Renamer global mappings now use minecraft.dependency.toSrgFile, matching the official FG7
  obfuscation example for Minecraft 1.20.4 and older.
- Keeps runtime Mixin refmap remapping enabled for AE2 soft references.

Reason:
Plain implementation left AE2's production SRG hard references untouched in the dev environment.
That caused PickColorMixin to fail on @Shadow field f_91074_ while targeting the official-mapped
net.minecraft.client.Minecraft class.

Test:
  .\gradlew.bat clean
  .\gradlew.bat runClient
