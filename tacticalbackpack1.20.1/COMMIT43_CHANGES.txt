TacticalBackpack commit43
=========================

Fixes the commit42 compilation failure in BackpackPocketChunkLoader.

Changes:
- BackpackPocketDimension.getPocketPos(...) is now called with the actual
  Tactical Backpack ItemStack instead of Player.
- Resolves the interior backpack by TB_InteriorBackpackId first, then falls
  back to the player's carried Tactical Backpack.
- Forces/releases the chunk in ModDimensions.BACKPACK_INNER_WORLD directly,
  rather than accidentally using the player's current dimension.
- Keeps backpack-per-room positioning intact.

Expected result:
- The two "Player cannot be converted to ItemStack" compile errors are gone.
- Existing seven deprecation/removal messages remain warnings only.
