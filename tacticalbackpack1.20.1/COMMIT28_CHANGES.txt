TacticalBackpack commit28

Capacity/overflow foundation
- Added configurable maximum internal storage slot count (default 50000).
- Size Upgrade I/II slot bonuses are now configurable (defaults 27 / 54).
- BackpackInventory clamps upgraded capacity to maxStorageSlots instead of a hard-coded limit.
- Added automatic-overflow policy config foundation:
  - enableAutoOverflow
  - overflowKeepFreeSlots
  - overflowToAE2
- Added BackpackOverflowPolicy as a loader-safe central policy for upcoming provider adapters.
- Nested backpack capacity accounting now respects configured maximum.

Important
- TacticalBackpackMenu currently exposes up to 54 storage slots at once.
- A paged 54-slots-per-page UI is the next required step before very large internal capacities are practical.
- commit28 does not yet automatically move picked-up items into AE2; it establishes the config/policy layer for that routing.
