TacticalBackpack commit36
=========================

Capacity attachments in the Attachments tab:
- SIZE_UPGRADE_1 and SIZE_UPGRADE_2 are now valid attachment items.
- They can be installed by drag-and-drop or Shift-click into the 18 attachment slots.
- Capacity bonus is active only while the upgrade is installed.
- Capacity still uses sizeUpgrade1Bonus / sizeUpgrade2Bonus and maxStorageSlots config values.
- Attachment storage is moved to a fixed BackpackAttachments NBT list, separate from the variable-size main inventory.
- This prevents attachment slots from shifting/disappearing when the capacity changes.
- Nested backpack attachment checks use the same stable attachment storage.
